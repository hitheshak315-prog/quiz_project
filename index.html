How QuizNest Works

A streamlined, frictionless testing experience from start to finish.

1

Enter Username & Select Test

Set your candidate name and choose between Easy, Medium, or Hard tests or tailored subjects.

2

Take the Live Mock Test

Answer randomized questions, navigate back & forth, mark tricky ones for review, and watch the countdown timer.

3

Get Instant Results & Review

View automatic scores, unlock the top performer trophy, and inspect detailed explanations for every mistake.

4

Track Analytics & Retake

Monitor your performance trends on the dashboard and compete on the local college leaderboard.

Key Highlights

Engineered for Better Learning

🔀

Double Randomization

Questions and 4 answer choices are dynamically shuffled every time to prevent pattern guessing.

💾

Continuous Answer State

Your selected choices are saved in memory while navigating between questions so nothing is lost.

⭐

Mark for Review

Flag questions you are unsure about and easily revisit them before your final submission.

⏱️

Auto-Submit Timer

Live countdown with pulse warning below 60s, automatically submitting tests at 00:00.

📊

Comprehensive Analytics

Aggregated test counts, average scores, accuracy rates, and historical logs in localStorage.

🏆

Top Performer Badges

Earn gold trophies when scoring 80% or above and see your name on the podium leaderboard.
